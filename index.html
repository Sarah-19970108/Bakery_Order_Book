How it works

  1. 1

    Add the order

    Whoever takes it fills in one short form and says how it came in: phone, email, Instagram, WhatsApp or walk-in.

  2. 2

    Answer the allergy question

    It can't be skipped. The order won't save until it says “none” or names the allergen.

  3. 3

    Follow it to the door

    Urgent events sit at the top. Move each order from new to baking, packed and collected.
